Iànua is the business assistant your company runs on its own infrastructure.

This iPhone app connects to your organisation's Iànua workspace and brings the tools your team already uses on the desktop to the phone, over authenticated and tenant-isolated connections.

What you can do:
- Chat with your company's AI agents and with your colleagues, in channels, groups, and direct conversations
- Send replies, reactions, attachments, and voice messages, and pin what matters
- Use the internal phone: call colleagues and external numbers through your company's phone system, with presence and do-not-disturb
- Approve sensitive operations with the built-in Authenticator, confirming each request with Face ID and a match code
- Activate the device in seconds by scanning a one-shot QR activation code issued by your administrator

Built for companies:
- Every workspace is isolated: data, conversations, and agents stay bound to your organisation
- Access is granted by your administrator and can be revoked at any time
- Security-relevant approvals are signed by a key held in the Secure Enclave of this iPhone
- Operations are recorded in your organisation's audit trail

Iànua requires an account on an existing Iànua workspace provided by your organisation. It is not a standalone consumer app.
